Measuring paper folding

Munns & Hegarty (2026, in prep)

An item response theory (IRT) analysis of three paper-folding measures:

  1. About 500 participants took each test separately.
  2. IRT models were fit to each test.
  3. The best-performing items were combined into a hybrid test.
  4. The hybrid test was given to a new sample of 500 participants.

Tests covered: Paper Folding Test (PFT), Differential Aptitude Test: Space Relations (DAT), and Surface Development Test (SD).
