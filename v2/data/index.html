PFT

Paper Folding Test

Prolific · January 2024 · n = 499 · 20 items

All 20 items of the Paper Folding Test, taken online by a Prolific sample. Item-by-item accuracy and response times, with demographics. Mean score 12.5 of 20 (SD 4.5).

Not screened for low effort (see the attention_check column) or for fast responses.
